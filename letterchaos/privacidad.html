Política de privacidad

LetterChaos es un juego de palabras desarrollado por Javier Extremera, desarrollador individual («yo»). Esta política explica qué datos usa el juego, para qué y con quién se comparten. Se aplica a la aplicación de Android y a la versión web del juego.

Lo que no recojo

El juego no pide registro, correo electrónico, contraseña, nombre real, ubicación, contactos, fotos ni micrófono. No vendo datos personales.

Notificaciones

En Android, el juego puede programar un recordatorio diario del ranked. Se genera en el propio dispositivo, no envía datos y se puede desactivar en Ajustes.

Base legal y conservación

Los datos del juego (alias, puntuaciones, salas) se tratan para darte el servicio que pides al jugar al ranked o al versus. Los anuncios personalizados se basan en tu consentimiento. Las salas de versus se borran a las 24 horas; las puntuaciones del ranked se mantienen mientras exista la clasificación. Los datos locales se borran al desinstalar el juego.

Niños

El juego no está dirigido a menores de 13 años y no recojo a sabiendas datos de menores de esa edad.

Tus derechos

Puedes pedirme acceso, corrección o borrado de tu alias y puntuaciones escribiendo al correo de abajo e indicando tu alias. También puedes reclamar ante la autoridad de protección de datos (en España, la AEPD).

Privacy policy

LetterChaos is a word game developed by Javier Extremera, an individual developer ("I"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Android app and to the web version of the game.

What I do not collect

The game does not ask for sign-up, email, password, real name, location, contacts, photos or microphone. I do not sell personal data.

Data the game uses

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Player name (a nickname you choose or generate at random) Show it on the daily leaderboard and to your opponent in versus mode On your device and, if you play ranked or versus, in the services listed below
Daily ranked scoresPublic daily leaderboardSilentWolf
Versus match data (room code, nickname, points, a random session identifier) Match two players and show the opponent's score Firebase Realtime Database (Google). Rooms are deleted automatically after 24 hours
Settings, statistics and streakRemember your preferences and progress Only on your device
Advertising ID, IP address and device information Show and measure ads and prevent fraudGoogle AdMob (Android only)

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads, Android only). It may collect the advertising ID, IP address and device data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it in Settings → "Ad privacy". Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy · how Google uses data from apps.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google), for versus mode: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • SilentWolf, for the daily leaderboard: silentwolf.com.

These services may process data outside your country (for example, in the United States), with the safeguards described in their policies.

Notifications

On Android the game can schedule a daily ranked reminder. It is created on the device itself, sends no data and can be turned off in Settings.

Legal basis and retention

Game data (nickname, scores, rooms) is processed to provide the service you request when you play ranked or versus. Personalised ads are based on your consent. Versus rooms are deleted after 24 hours; ranked scores are kept as long as the leaderboard exists. Local data is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your nickname and scores by writing to the email below and telling me your nickname. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
